Join the **ELEPHANT crew** on **21 November 2026, from 18:00**, at **Center Zalog in Ljubljana** for another evening of cinema, music and storytelling at **ELEPHANT Movie Night 2026**. This year's programme brings together three short fiction films, each exploring the complexities of family, relationships and the truths we struggle to face.

The evening begins with a welcome from the team and a short live piano performance, followed by screenings of **Autumn Ballad**, **An Unpleasant Incident** and **Something Red**. Afterwards, stay for an informal gathering and the chance to connect with the filmmakers and fellow film lovers.

## Featured Films

- **Autumn Ballad (Jesenska balada)** — *16 min*  
  As he feels anxious about the remainder of his life, elderly Anton seeks solace in his wife’s presence. Does he even have a choice?  
  *Written and directed by Irena Žugič.*

- **The Unfortunate Incident (Neprijetni dogodek)** — *23 min*  
  After Mimi is sexually harassed at school, her mother tries everything in her power to protect her, but nothing is as it seems.  
  *Directed by Jan Fabris; written by Jan Fabris and Lev Mastnak Trobentar.*

- **Something Red (Nekaj rdečega)** — *28 min*  
  After her mother passed away, Sandra unexpectedly discovers a strange family secret that makes her re-examine her own identity.  
  *Directed by Jan Fabris; written by Jan Fabris and Suzana Kokalj.*

## Evening Programme

- **18:00** — Welcome and short live piano performance
- **18:20** — Screening of the three short films
- **19:30** — Informal gathering and conversations
- **21:00** — End of the event
